# Code of Conduct

This project should be a friendly place for everyone. By taking part, you agree to help keep it that way.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Assume good intent and give useful, kind feedback.
- Accept that people have different skill levels and points of view.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or offensive comments.
- Sharing someone's private information without their consent.
- Anything that would be out of place in a professional setting.

## Reporting

If you see or experience unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the maintainer, Alok Tomar, on X at https://x.com/AlokTom19637918. Reports are handled discreetly.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, and pull requests that break these rules, and may block anyone who keeps breaking them.
